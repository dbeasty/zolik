# Oko bere

The Czech version of twenty-one, played with German-suited cards against a
bank that passes round the table. Played for chips, never real money. The app
marks it "18+", and on phones it's hidden from players under 18.

- **Players:** 2–6
- **Variation:** Classic
- **Bots:** Easy, Medium, Hard
- **In-app rules:** the game's page › **Rules ›**

## Objective

Get closer to 21 than the bank without going over, and win its chips. The
player with the most chips at the end wins.

## The cards

32 German-suited cards, shuffled again every round.

| Card | Counts |
| --- | --- |
| 7, 8, 9, 10 | Their number |
| Spodek (Unter), svršek (Ober) | 1 |
| Král (King) | 2 |
| Eso (Ace) | 11 |

Two aces as your first two cards are **oko** ("the eye"), and win at once.

## The bank

One player holds the bank and stakes 50 chips in it (by default), or all they
have. All the stakes on a round together can be no more than the bank. The
bank passes to the left after 5 rounds (by default), or at once if it's
broken.

## How a round works

1. Everyone gets one card face down. The punters (everyone except the banker)
   play in turn from the banker's left, and the banker plays last.
2. Look at your card and **stake** at least the table minimum, but no more
   than your chips or what the bank can still cover.
3. Take cards one at a time, and stop when you have enough.
4. Over 21 is **bust**: your stake goes to the bank at once.
5. A punter's oko is paid three times the stake at once (by default).
6. The banker then turns up their cards and draws in the same way.

## Settling

- Against a bank of 21 or under, a punter with more wins the stake, and with
  the same or less loses it.
- A bust bank pays everyone still standing.
- A banker's oko wins every stake still on the table.

Everyone starts with 100 chips (by default). The match ends when the bank has
gone round the table the set number of times, and the most chips wins.

## Options

| Option | Choices | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Starting chips** | 100 / 200 / 500 | 100 | Chips each player starts with. |
| **Table minimum** | 1 / 2 / 5 | 2 | The smallest stake a punter may put against the bank. |
| **Bank** | 20 / 50 / 100 | 50 | What a banker stakes when the bank comes to them. |
| **Rounds per bank** | 3 / 5 / 8 | 5 | How many rounds a banker holds the bank, unless it's broken first. |
| **Times round the table** | 1 / 2 / 3 | 1 | How many times the bank goes round before the match ends. |
| **Two aces pay** | The stake / Three times | Three times | What a punter's oko collects. |

Also available: Opponents, Pause between rounds, When a player drops, Step
out, Hints.

## Tips

- Ties go to the bank, so a punter needs to *beat* the bank's total.
- The face cards are low here (1 or 2), and the ace is 11. Count carefully.
