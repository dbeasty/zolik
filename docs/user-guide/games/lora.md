# Lóra

A Czech compendium game for four. Each player in turn leads a **tálie**, a
fixed sequence of seven different games, almost all about *avoiding* penalty
points.

- **Players:** exactly 4
- **Variation:** Classic
- **Bots:** Easy, Medium, Hard
- **In-app rules:** the game's page › **Rules ›**

The app keeps the traditional Czech names for the cards and the games.

## Objective

Take as few penalty points as possible. The fewest at the end of the match
wins.

## The cards

32 German-suited cards, ranked eso, král, svršek, spodek, 10, 9, 8, 7. There
are no trumps. Each player gets 8 cards.

## The tálie

A tálie is seven games in a fixed order, all led by the same player. The next
tálie is led by the next player. A full match is 4 tálie, so everyone leads
one.

| # | Game | Penalty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Červené** (reds) | Each red card you take costs 1. |
| 2 | **Filky** | Each svršek you take costs 2. |
| 3 | **První–poslední** (first and last) | The first and the last trick cost 4 each. |
| 4 | **Všechny** (all) | Each trick you take costs 1. |
| 5 | **Červený král** (red king) | Taking the red král costs 8. |
| 6 | **Kvarty** | Each card left in hand when someone goes out costs 1. |
| 7 | **Desítky** (tens) | Each card left in hand costs 1, and each "ťuk" costs 1. |

### The trick games (1–5)

Follow suit if you can, otherwise play any card. The highest card of the suit
led takes the trick, and its winner leads next. In červené and červený král
you may lead a red card only when you hold no other suit. Červené, filky and
červený král end as soon as every penalty card has been taken.

### Kvarty

The leader starts a quart with any card. The quart is that card and the three
above it in its suit, or fewer if it reaches the eso. Whoever holds the next
card of the quart must lay it, until the quart is complete or its next card is
already gone. Whoever laid its highest card starts the next quart. Kvarty ends
when a player has laid all their cards.

### Desítky

On your turn, lay as many cards as you like, one at a time. A 10 starts its
suit's row, which grows one card at a time down to the 7 and up to the eso.
If you can lay a card, you must lay at least one. If you can lay nothing, say
**ťuk**, which costs 1. Desítky ends when a player has laid all their cards.

### Maturita

With **Maturita** played (the default), the leader of each tálie ends it
with a *maturita*: a game of their choice, which they must finish without a
single penalty point. They choose after seeing their cards. Any game is
allowed except první–poslední, which only a third attempt may choose. Only
the maturant scores. A penalty point ends the attempt and costs the maturant
8, and they try again with a new deal. A third failure ends the match, and the
maturant finishes last.

## Options

| Option | Choices | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Tálie** | 1 / 2 / 4 | 4 | How many tálie the match lasts. |
| **Maturita** | Not played / Played | Played | End each tálie with the leader's maturita. |

Also available: Opponents, Pause between rounds, When a player drops, Step
out, Hints.

## Tips

- In červené and červený král, get rid of your red cards early by discarding
  them when you can't follow suit.
- In desítky, hold back cards that would open a row for your opponents. Lay
  only what you must.
- Choose your maturita game carefully. All seven are allowed except
  první–poslední, so pick the one your hand can play cleanly.
