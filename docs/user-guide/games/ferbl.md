# Ferbl

A Czech four-card betting game with German-suited cards. Hold the best four
cards, or bet as if you do until the others fold. Played for chips, never real
money. The app marks it "18+", and on phones it's hidden from players under
18.

- **Players:** 2–6
- **Variation:** Classic
- **Bots:** Easy, Medium, Hard
- **In-app rules:** the game's page › **Rules ›**

## The cards

32 German-suited cards. Each player gets four, dealt two and two.

| Card | Counts |
| --- | --- |
| Eso (Ace) | 11 |
| Král, svršek, spodek, 10 | 10 |
| 9, 8, 7 | Their number |

## Hands, best first

1. **Four of a kind**: eso highest, seven lowest.
2. **Banda**: four of a suit, ranked by its total (41 down to 34).
3. **Three of a kind.**
4. **Three of a suit**, ranked by their total (31 down to 24).
5. **Two aces.**
6. **Two of a suit**, ranked by their total (21 down to 15).
7. **One card of each suit**, ranked by the highest card.

Cards outside the hand's own combination count for nothing. Equal hands go to
the player first in turn from the dealer's left.

## Betting

1. Everyone pays the **vizi** (the ante, 2 by default) before the deal, and
   gets two cards.
2. **First round**, on two cards, starting from the dealer's left: check or
   bet one vizi. Once there's a bet: call, raise by one vizi, or fold.
3. Two more cards each, then a **second round** with bets and raises of two
   vizi.
4. Each round closes after 3 raises.
5. A player short of chips may still call with what they have, and then plays
   only for the part of the pot they matched.

## Showdown

When the betting ends, the hands still in are shown and the best takes the
pot. If everyone else folds, the last player takes it without showing.

Everyone starts with 100 chips (by default). The match lasts 20 hands, or
until only one player has chips, and the most chips wins.

## Options

| Option | Choices | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Starting chips** | 100 / 200 / 500 | 100 | Chips each player starts with. |
| **Vizi** | 1 / 2 / 5 | 2 | The ante every player pays each hand, and the bet on two cards. A bet on four is twice it. |
| **Hands** | 10 / 20 / 30 | 20 | How many hands the match lasts, unless only one player has chips left. |

Also available: Opponents, Pause between rounds, When a player drops, Step
out, Hints.

## Tips

- Two aces beat any two-of-a-suit, but lose to three of a suit. Know where you
  stand before the second round's bigger bets.
- Position matters: equal hands go to whoever is first from the dealer's
  left.
