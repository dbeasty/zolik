# Solitaire

Klondike, the classic patience game. You play alone against the deck.

- **Players:** 1
- **Variations:** Classic, Draw Three, Vegas
- **In-app rules:** the game's page › **Rules ›**

Start a game from the Solitaire page with **Deal me in**.

## The deal

Seven columns are dealt with one to seven cards each, with only the top card
of each face up. The other 24 cards are the **stock**. The stock turns onto
the **waste**: one card at a time in Classic, or three at a time in Draw Three
and Vegas, when only the top one can be played.

## Playing

- **Columns** build down in alternating colours, such as a red six on a black
  seven.
- Any face-up run moves as one, from the card you pick up to the bottom of
  the column.
- Only a king, or a run headed by one, can fill an empty column.
- A face-down card left on top of a column turns up by itself.
- The four **foundations** build up by suit, from ace to king.
- By default, a card may come back down from a foundation onto a column.
- When the stock is empty, the waste can be turned back over to make a new
  stock: as often as you like in Classic and Draw Three, and never in Vegas.
- **Undo** takes back a move, but never a card turned up or drawn.
- Once every card is face up, **Finish** plays the rest out for you.

## Scoring

| Scoring | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Standard** (Classic, Draw Three) | Waste to column 5, any card to a foundation 10, turning a card up 5, a card back off a foundation −15. Turning the waste over costs 100 (20 when drawing three). |
| **Vegas** | You start at −52, and every card on a foundation is worth 5. |
| **None** | The game isn't scored. |

## How the game ends

You win when all 52 cards are on the foundations. You can give up at any
time, and the game counts as lost. The game also ends by itself when nothing
is left that could change it, or after 250 moves in a row that got nowhere.
An unfinished game ends with **Not solved**.

**Play this deal again** replays the same cards, and **Send this deal** lets a
friend try the exact deal you just played. The list of others who played it
shows who **solved** it.

## Options

| Option | Choices | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cards turned** | One / Three | Classic: One; Draw Three, Vegas: Three | How many cards the stock turns over at a time. |
| **Turning the waste over** | Unlimited / Twice / Never | Classic, Draw Three: Unlimited; Vegas: Never | How many times the waste may become the stock again. |
| **Scoring** | Standard / Vegas / None | Classic, Draw Three: Standard; Vegas: Vegas | How the game is scored. |
| **Cards back from the foundations** | Allowed / Not allowed | Allowed | Whether a card may move down from a foundation to a column. |
| **Finish for me** | Offered / Not offered | Offered | Offer to play out the last cards once every card is face up. |
| **Hints** | Allowed / Off | Allowed | Ask for a suggested move. |

## Tips

- Turn up face-down cards first. A move that reveals a card is usually better
  than one that doesn't.
- Don't empty a column unless you have a king ready to move into it.
- Solitaire is dealt by the online server, so it may not be offered at an
  offline table.
