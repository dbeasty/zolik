# Last Card

A fast colour-matching card game with action cards and wilds. Match the top
card, hit the next player with draw cards, and remember to call "Last card!"

- **Players:** 2–10
- **Variation:** Classic
- **Bots:** Easy, Medium, Hard
- **In-app rules:** the game's page › **Rules ›**

## The pack

A 112-card pack in four colours (coral, teal, violet and amber). Each colour
has the numbers 0 to 9 plus **Skip**, **Reverse** and **Draw Two** cards. There
are also **Wilds** and **Wild Draw Fours**, and, with the default house rules,
two **Wild Swap** and two **Wild Mayhem** cards. Each player starts with 7
cards.

## How a turn works

Play one card that matches the top card by colour, by number or by symbol,
or play a Wild. If you don't play, draw one card. If it fits, you may play it
straight away or keep it, and either way your turn then ends. (With **Draw
until you can play** on, you keep drawing until a playable card turns up.)

## Action cards and wilds

| Card | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Skip** | The next player misses their turn. |
| **Reverse** | Play changes direction. With two players it works like a Skip. |
| **Draw Two** | The next player draws two cards and misses their turn. |
| **Wild** | Goes on anything. Name the colour that continues. |
| **Wild Draw Four** | Name the colour, and the next player draws four and misses their turn. It's only honest when you hold no card of the colour in play, but you may bluff. |
| **Wild Swap** | Name the colour that follows, then trade hands with any player you choose. |
| **Wild Mayhem** | Name the colour that follows, then every hand is gathered, shuffled and dealt out again one card at a time, starting with the next player. |

### "Last card!"

When you hold two cards, say **Last card!** before you play the second-to-last
one. If you forget, the next player may catch you before they move, and you
draw two cards.

### Challenging a Wild Draw Four

Hit by a Wild Draw Four, you may challenge it. If it was a bluff, its player
draws the four instead. If it was honest, you draw six and miss your turn.
Either way, you see the hand it came from.

## Scoring

By default, whoever goes out scores the cards left in everyone else's hands.
Number cards score their number. Skip, Reverse and Draw Two score 20, and
wilds score 50. The first player to reach 500 wins.

With **Scoring** set to "Lowest total wins", everyone pays for their own cards
instead, and the lowest total wins. **Target score** can also be set to
"One deal".

## Players who are away

By default, a player whose connection drops is dealt out of the next deals
until they return, and scores nothing meanwhile. A bot finishes the deal in
play for them. With **While a player is away** set to "A bot keeps playing",
the bot goes on playing for them.

## Options

| Option | Choices | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Target score** | One deal / 200 / 500 | 500 | Play deal after deal until someone's total reaches this, or play a single deal. |
| **Scoring** | Winner takes the points / Lowest total wins | Winner takes the points | See Scoring. |
| **Calling "Last card!"** | On / Off | On | Say it before the play that leaves you one card, or be caught for two cards. |
| **Wild Draw Four challenge** | On / Off | On | A Wild Draw Four may be bluffed, and the next player may challenge it. |
| **Stacking draw cards** | Off / Draw Two on Draw Two / Any draw card | Off | Answer a draw card with one of your own and pass the whole stack on. Whoever can't takes it all and misses their turn. |
| **Draw until you can play** | Off / On | Off | Keep drawing until a playable card turns up. |
| **Sevens and zeros** | Off / On | Off | A 7 swaps your hand with a player you choose; a 0 passes every hand along to the next player. |
| **Wild Swap cards** | Off / On | On | Adds the two Wild Swaps. |
| **Wild Mayhem cards** | Off / On | On | Adds the two Wild Mayhems. |
| **While a player is away** | Deal them out / A bot keeps playing | Deal them out | See above. |
| **Cards dealt** | 5 / 7 / 10 | 7 | How many cards each player starts with. |
| **Discard pile** | Open to look through / Top card only | Top card only | Whether players may look through the whole pile. |

Also available: Pause between rounds, Opponents, When a player drops, Step
out, Hints.

## Tips

- Save a Wild Draw Four for when you really hold none of the colour in play.
  A failed challenge costs your opponent six cards.
- Watch hand sizes. A Wild Swap with the player who's about to go out can
  turn a deal around.
- Turn on the **Voice announcer** in Settings to hear "Take 2", "Skip" and
  "Last card!" called out.
