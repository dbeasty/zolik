# Prší

The Czech pub classic, played with a 32-card German-suited deck (hearts,
bells, acorns and leaves, from 7 up to the ace). Match the top card, use the
special cards against the next player, and be first to empty your hand.

- **Players:** 2–6
- **Variation:** Classic
- **Bots:** Easy, Medium, Hard
- **In-app rules:** the game's page › **Rules ›**

## Objective

Be the first to play every card in your hand. The match ends the moment
someone's hand is empty.

## Setup

Each player is dealt 4 cards (5 or 6 with the **Cards dealt** option). One
card is turned up to start the discard pile, and the rest are the stock. The
first card's effect applies to the first player, exactly as if it had been
played at them.

## How a turn works

Play one card that matches the top card's **suit** or **rank**, or draw a
card if you can't (or don't want to). Drawing ends your turn without a play.
When the stock runs out, the discard pile, except its top card, is shuffled
to make a new stock.

## Special cards

| Card | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **7** | The next player draws two cards, unless they answer with a 7 of their own. A 7 played on a 7 passes the whole debt on, two cards bigger. |
| **Ace** | The next player misses their turn, unless they answer with an ace of their own, which passes the skip on. |
| **Svršek** (the Ober) | Goes on any card. Name the suit that continues. The next card must be of that suit. |

A card that empties your hand wins at once, so a 7 played as your last card
wins rather than making anyone draw.

## Options

| Option | Choices | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cards dealt** | 4 / 5 / 6 | 4 | How many cards each player starts with. |
| **Discard pile** | Open to look through / Top card only | Top card only | Whether players may look through the whole pile. |

Also available: Opponents, When a player drops, Step out, Hints.

## Tips

- Keep a 7 or an ace back to answer one played at you.
- Use the svršek to switch to a suit you have plenty of, or one you think
  the next player lacks.
- The status line names what you can do ("Play a hearts card, or one that
  matches …, otherwise draw.").
