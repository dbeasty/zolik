# Sedma

A Czech trick game with no suits to follow, where any seven ("sedma")
captures. Take the aces and tens.

- **Players:** 2–4 (four play in two partnerships, partners opposite; two or
  three play alone)
- **Variation:** Classic
- **Bots:** Easy, Medium, Hard
- **In-app rules:** the game's page › **Rules ›**

## Objective

Take aces (eso) and tens in tricks. Each is worth 10, and the last trick 10
more: 90 points in a hand. Every other card is worth nothing.

## The cards

32 German-suited cards. With three players, two eights are taken out,
leaving 30.

## How a hand works

1. Each player gets 4 cards, and the rest is the stock. The player left of the
   dealer leads, and the deal passes to the left each hand.
2. **There's no suit to follow:** play any card.
3. A card of the **rank led**, or **any 7**, captures the trick. The last one
   played takes it.
4. When everyone has played, the leader may **continue** the trick with
   another card of the rank led or a 7, and everyone plays again. Otherwise
   the trick ends.
5. After each trick everyone draws from the stock, the winner first, back to
   4 cards while the stock lasts.

## Scoring

- The side with the most points wins **1** stake, **2** if it took all 90
  points, and **3** if it took every card.
- With three players, two players level on most points win 1 each. At 30
  each, nobody scores.
- The first to 5 stakes (by default) wins the match.

## Options

| Option | Choices | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Play to** | 3 / 5 / 10 | 5 | How many stakes win the match. |
| **Card points** | Hidden until the end / Shown as you play | Shown as you play | Show every side's points as the hand is played. Your own side's are always shown to you. |

Also available: Opponents, Pause between rounds, When a player drops, Step
out, Hints.

## Tips

- Sevens are your trumps. Save them for tricks that hold an ace or a ten.
- If you lead and get captured, think about continuing the trick to win it
  back, but only if you hold the cards to do it.
