# Offline play and phones

The Jokerless apps for iPhone, Android and Mac carry their own copy of the
game server. That lets a phone or a Mac host a table by itself, with no
internet connection, for you alone against bots or for everyone in the room.

**Where it's available:** the iPhone, iPad, Android and Mac apps. The web
version at jokerless.com can't host an offline table, but a browser can
*join* one that a phone in the same room is hosting (see below).

## Playing offline against bots

1. Open **Play offline**. With no internet it's on the main screen. When
   you're online it's on each game's page.
2. The screen reads: "This device hosts the table itself, so you can play
   against bots anywhere — even in airplane mode."
3. Enter **Your name at the table** and press **Start an offline table**.
4. **Choose a game**, set it up as usual, and play.

While you're at your device's offline table, the app says "You are at this
device's offline table. Games you start here stay on this device." Press
**Back to online play** to leave.

Solitaire is dealt only by the online server, so it may be missing from the
games an offline table offers.

## Playing with people in the same room

Once your device is hosting, it can let others in.

### Over Wi-Fi or a hotspot

Press **Invite players nearby**: "Let other devices on this Wi-Fi or hotspot
join your table. No internet needed." **Tell players nearby** (on by
default) opens this automatically when you start hosting, and offers your
table to devices in the room that have the app open.

Other players can join in three ways:

- **With the app:** they open **Play offline** and press **Join** under
  **Tables nearby**. If nothing shows up: "Make sure you are on the same
  Wi-Fi or hotspot as the host, and that Jokerless may use the local
  network."
- **By address:** the host's screen shows an address. They type it under
  **Join by address**.
- **In a browser, no app needed:** they scan the QR code with a phone camera
  ("Scan with a phone camera to play in the browser. No app needed."). The
  page explains: "This table is running on a phone in this room. Play here in
  your browser, nothing to install."

Press **Stop inviting** to close the room to new players.

### Over Bluetooth

For devices that share no Wi-Fi: the host presses **Invite over Bluetooth**
("No pairing needed"). Guests press **Look for tables over Bluetooth**. Each
guest is shown a **Check code**, which should match what the host's screen
shows next to that guest. Bluetooth has to be switched on and allowed for
Jokerless in your device's settings. The Bluetooth controls don't appear on
devices that can't use them.

### Over the internet, from your phone

Under **Anywhere**, a signed-in host can press **Let people join over the
internet**: "Let people anywhere join this table with a link. Your device
stays the server." Press **Share the link** to send it. Friends open the link
and press **Join the table**. **Close to the internet** stops it. This needs
you to be signed in ("Sign in to let people join over the internet.").

## What happens when the host goes away

The game lives on the host's device. If that device sleeps, leaves the app or
loses its connection, "the game pauses for everyone and carries on when it's
back." Players see "The server ({name}) is offline. The game is paused and
nothing is lost."

If you try to join a table running a different version of Jokerless: "Update
the app on both devices."

## Keeping offline games

A game played at a table on a phone stays on that phone unless its players
say otherwise, each for their own seat. When the game ends:

- The host is asked "Save this game to your Jokerless account?" A saved game
  is uploaded the next time the phone is online, once everyone at the table
  has answered.
- Each guest is asked whether to keep the game for their own account. "This
  is only about your own seat. Anyone who doesn't say yes appears as an
  anonymous player."
- A player in a browser gets a link to keep. "Open it when you're online and
  sign in to add the game to your account."

**Games on this device** (on the Play offline screen) lists hosted games that
are "Not saved yet" or "Saved, waiting for a connection". You can **Save**
them or **Forget it**. Under "When a game I hosted ends" you choose **Ask me
whether to save it**, **Always save it to my account** or **Never save it,
and don't ask**. Saving needs you to be signed in on that device.

## Phone-specific notes

- **Screen rotation:** Settings lets you lock the screen to **Portrait** or
  **Landscape**, or **Follow phone**.
- **Notifications:** turn on **Notifications on this device** in Settings to
  hear about table invites while the app is closed.
- **Nearby tables:** with **Nearby tables** on, a banner appears when a
  phone near you opens a table, while the app is open.
- **Age:** on iPhone and Android, the app asks the operating system for the
  player's age range. Chip games (Poker, Blackjack, Oko bere, Ferbl and
  Siete y Medio) are hidden from players under 18. Anyone following a link
  to one sees "For players 18 and over". The web version doesn't ask.
- **Dragging cards:** on a touch screen, hold a card briefly before dragging
  it.
