# Getting started

## The first screen

The first time you open Jokerless you see a short welcome: "Classic card
games, played online with real people". It has two buttons, **Play** and
**Join a table**. Press **Play** to go to the games list, or **Join a table**
if a friend has given you a table code.

At the bottom of the games list there's a **Take a quick tour ›** link. The
tour walks you through the games list, a game's own page, the table setup
screen, the "Learn to play" lessons, the **More** menu and **Settings**. You
can replay it at any time.

## Guest or account?

You need one of two things before you can play online:

- **Continue as guest.** No account, no email, nothing to install on the
  web. You pick a display name and a face, and you're in.
- **Sign in.** Your statistics, leaderboard place, score tables and game
  circle are kept with your account, and they follow you to any device you
  sign in on.

What a guest can and can't do:

| | Guest | Account |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Play every game, with friends or bots | Yes | Yes |
| Open tables, invite people, join by code or link | Yes | Yes |
| **My games** (resume unfinished tables, replay finished ones) | Yes | Yes |
| **Game circle** (be told when friends open a table) | Yes | Yes |
| **Stats & leaderboard** | No | Yes |
| **Record a live game** (a scorecard for real cards) | No | Yes |
| A record kept at the end of each match | No | Yes |
| Your face and table look follow you to another device | No, they stay on this device | Yes |

Stats and score tables are greyed out for guests with the note "(sign in to
use)". If you open one anyway, the app explains: "Score tables and stats are
kept with your account, so they follow you to another device. A guest has
nowhere for them to be kept."

### Playing as a guest

1. On the games list, press **Continue as guest** (or press any game: you'll
   be asked to choose a guest identity first).
2. The **Guest play** screen ("No account required") suggests a display
   name. Change it if you like, and pick **Your face at the table**.
3. Press **Continue**.

Your guest identity is a random key stored on your device. Come back on the
same device and browser and you're the same guest, with the same name, face
and unfinished games.

### Keeping your guest identity on another device

Your guest identity belongs to the device it was created on. To carry it
somewhere else:

1. Open the account menu (your face in the top corner) and choose
   **Account**.
2. Under **Your guest link**, press **Copy link** (on the web) or
   **Share link** (in the app).
3. Open that link on the other device. It shows a **Guest link** screen.
   Press **Continue as this guest**.

The app warns you about the link: "Keep it to yourself: whoever has it plays
as you." If you're already signed in to an account on the other device, sign
out first, because a guest link can't be used while you're signed in. Any
guest games already on that other device stay with its old guest identity.

If you lose the device and never made a copy of the link, the guest identity
can't be recovered. If you play regularly, an account is the safer choice.

## Signing in

Press **Sign in** on the games list, in **Settings**, or in the account
menu. The **Sign in** screen ("Keep your statistics across devices") offers:

- **Continue with Google** and **Continue with Apple**, on servers where
  these are set up.
- **Email me a sign-in code.** Enter your email address and press
  **Send code**. Then type the 6-digit code from the email and press
  **Continue**. No password is involved. The code is only valid for a short
  time.
- **Sign in with a username instead.** This is for username and password
  accounts. The same screen has **Create a username/password account**,
  which asks for a **Username**, an optional email and a **Password**.

The legal notice above the buttons tells you what you're agreeing to by
signing in.

### Keeping the games you played as a guest

If you played as a guest on this device before signing in, the sign-in
screen says something like "You have 3 games recorded on this device. Sign
in to keep them." After you sign in, the **Account** screen offers
**Keep these games**, which adds them to your account. The end-of-match
**Your record** panel has the same offer for a guest: **Sign in and keep
these**.

### Managing your account

The **Account** screen (account menu › **Account**) shows:

- **Signed in with**: each sign-in method linked to your account, with
  **Remove**.
- **Add a sign-in method**: link Google, Apple or another provider to the
  same account, so you can sign in either way.
- **Face and table look**: a shortcut to Settings.
- **Delete account**. See [Deleting your account](faq.md#deleting-your-account).

To sign out, open the account menu and choose **Sign out**.

## Your first table

The quickest way to play:

1. On the games list, under **Pick a game**, press the game you want.
2. On that game's page, press **Play now**. Bots fill the other seats, so
   the cards are dealt straight away.
3. If you'd rather learn first, press **Learn to play** for a guided lesson
   at a real table (see below).

To play with friends instead, press **Open a table**, choose the rules,
press **Open table**, and send the invite link. [Playing](playing.md)
covers all of this in detail.

### Learn to play

Every game has a hands-on lesson. **Learn to play** on the game's page
teaches the default rules. **Learn with these settings** on the setup screen
teaches exactly the variation and options you've picked. A lesson is a real
table with bots and a coach card that highlights each step: **Next**,
**Back**, **Read the rule**, and **Exit guide**. When you leave it you can
**Play a real game** with the same settings, **Keep practising**, or
**Leave the guide**. Finished lessons get a tick (✓) on their button.
Lessons don't count towards your statistics.
