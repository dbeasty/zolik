# Jokerless player guide

Jokerless is a collection of classic card games that you can play online
with friends, against computer opponents ("bots"), or both at the same
table. You can start without an account: play as a guest, and sign in later
if you want your statistics kept.

This guide is for players. It explains how to get started, how a table
works, how to play each game, and what to do when something goes wrong.

Poker, Blackjack, Oko bere, Ferbl and Siete y Medio are played for chips.
The app marks them "18+": "Simulated gambling: played for chips, never real
money, and meant for players 18 and over." No real money is ever involved,
and chips can't be bought.

Every game has its rules written out in the app. Open a game from the games
list and press **Rules ›**, or press the rules button on the table during a
match. The rules you see there follow the variation and options of the table
you're looking at, so they always describe the game you're actually playing.
The game pages in this guide summarise those rules. If the two ever disagree,
the in-app rules are the ones the game follows.

## Where you can play

| Platform | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Web browser | [jokerless.com](https://jokerless.com). Nothing to install. `jokerless.org` and `play.limidus.com` serve the same game. |
| iPhone and iPad | The Jokerless app. It can also host a table on the phone itself, with no internet (see [Offline play](offline-and-mobile.md)). |
| Android | The Jokerless app, with the same offline hosting as on iPhone. |
| Mac | The Jokerless Mac app. It has its own menu bar (File › New Game, View › Hide Hand/Table/Log, and so on) and can host an offline table too. |

Your account works on every platform. Sign in on a second device and your
statistics, game circle and unfinished games follow you. A guest identity
can be carried to another device as well: see
[Keeping your guest identity on another device](getting-started.md#keeping-your-guest-identity-on-another-device).

The game server also contains a text-only terminal client that you connect
to over SSH. It is a developer tool, and it is switched off on jokerless.com.

## Privacy in one paragraph

From the in-app Privacy Notice: Jokerless has no analytics, no advertising
and no tracking. It stores what the game needs: your display name and face,
your sign-in method, your results and statistics, the moves of your games
(so they can be resumed and replayed) and your game circle. You can delete
your account yourself from the **Account** screen. The full notices are in
**Settings › The small print**. See also the [FAQ](faq.md#privacy-and-your-data).
