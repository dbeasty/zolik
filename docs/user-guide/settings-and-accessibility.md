# Settings and accessibility

Open **Settings** from the account menu (your face in the top corner). In
the Mac app it's also under **Jokerless › Settings…**. Every choice takes
effect as soon as you make it. **Done** just closes the screen.

At the top, Settings tells you who you are: "Signed in as {name}", "Playing
as {name} (guest)" or "Not signed in".

## Your face at the table

Pick the face other players see next to your name. If you're signed in, it's
"Kept with your account, so it follows you to another device". For a guest,
it's "Kept on this device".

You can also change your name and face for a single table: press your own
seat on the match screen (**Change your name and face**).

## Table look

The colours of the felt, the cards and the controls:

- **Heirloom**
- **Casino**
- **Classic**
- **High contrast**

The look button on the match screen cycles through the same list. A look
only ever changes colours and decoration. Card and text sizes stay the same,
so the board is laid out identically under every look. For bigger cards, use
**Card size** under Accessibility.

## Language

Jokerless is translated into 24 languages: Bulgarian, Croatian, Czech,
Danish, Dutch, English, Estonian, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hungarian,
Irish, Italian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Maltese, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian,
Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish and Swedish.

**Automatic** "Uses the language your device is set to, and changes when you
change it". The picker shows which language that currently is. The language
setting is kept on this device.

The written rules, move descriptions and table options are all translated
too. Some games keep their traditional card and contract names (for
example the Czech names in Šnaps, Sedma, Lóra and Oko bere). A few newer
accessibility phrases may still appear in English. The Terms and the Privacy
Notice are available in fewer languages than the app.

## Screen rotation

On phones and tablets: **Follow phone**, **Portrait** or **Landscape**.
"Follow phone turns the screen with the phone." Kept on this device.

## Sound

- **Sound effects**: cards dropped, a pile taken, your turn, the end of a
  round or a game. The speaker button on the table turns this on and off
  too. On by default.
- **Voice announcer**: "Calls out plays aloud — 'Take 2', 'Skip', 'Last
  card'". It also calls out "Knock", "Gin!", "You win!" and "Game over".
  Off by default, and it needs sound effects to be on.

## Accessibility

"How the game speaks, looks and moves on this device."

| Setting | Choices | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Screen reader announcements** | Every move / My turn and results / Off | What a screen reader announces during a game. Default: Every move. |
| **Tooltips** | On / Off | "Explanations when you hover, focus or long-press a control or card." Default: on. |
| **Keyboard shortcuts** | On / Off | Single-key shortcuts on the game board. Default: on. |
| **Four-colour deck** | On / Off | "Diamonds blue and clubs green, so no two suits share a colour." Default: off. |
| **High contrast** | Automatic / On / Off | "Stronger colours and borders. Automatic follows your system setting." The game table changes at once; other screens change the next time the app opens. |
| **Card size** | Normal / Large / Extra large | Bigger cards on the table. |
| **Reduce motion** | Automatic / On / Off | "Cards move instantly instead of flying. Automatic follows your system setting." |
| **Confirm final moves** | On / Off | "Ask before a move that ends the hand, like going out." Default: off. |

### Screen readers

Jokerless works with VoiceOver, TalkBack, NVDA and other screen readers.
Outside a game, buttons are named, screens have headings, form fields are
labelled and errors are read out. On the game board:

- The board is split into regions: **Your hand**, **Table**, **Players**,
  **Actions** and **Recent moves**.
- Every card is read by name and position, for example "Queen of hearts,
  3 of 11, can be played". The reader also says whether the card was just
  drawn, is marked, and where it can go ("Can go to: …").
- **Read table** reads the whole table in one go: whose turn it is, the
  scores, the piles and your hand. On iOS, the magic tap does the same.
- **Repeat last move** says the last move again.
- Hover, focus or long-press a control and the tooltip is read out as well.

The in-app accessibility statement is honest about the limits: the game
board is "still being made fully accessible", and "Some games cannot yet be
played from start to finish with only a screen reader or only a keyboard".
The iOS and Android apps haven't yet been reviewed by people who use
assistive technology every day.

### Keyboard shortcuts

On the web, press **?** at a table to see this list. With **Keyboard
shortcuts** off, the list says so.

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| D | Draw from the face-down pile |
| T | Take from the face-up pile |
| Enter | Make the highlighted move |
| U | Undo |
| R | Read the table |
| M | Go to recent moves |
| Esc | Clear the selection, or close a sheet or tooltip |
| ? | Show this list |
| ← → | Move between the cards in your hand |
| Space | Select or deselect a card |
| Shift + ← → | Move a card along your hand |
| Enter (on a card) | Play a card, choosing where if it can go to more than one place |
| I | Why a card in your hand is marked |

Outside a game, every control can be reached with Tab and used with Enter or
Space. The arrow keys move between the choices in a group, and Escape closes
menus and tooltips.

### Text size

The screens outside a game follow your browser zoom and your device's text
size, and their text wraps rather than being cut off. On the board, use
**Card size** and the board's zoom buttons.

### Reporting a problem

The full statement is under **About › Accessibility statement**. It asks you
to report anything that doesn't work with your assistive technology, saying
what you were trying to do and which device, browser or app version you used.
**About** shows the version.

## Notifications

- **Game invites**: "Who may tell you when they open a table". Choose
  **My circle** or **Off**. Your circle is managed under **Game circle**.
- **Nearby tables**: "Show a banner when a phone near you opens a table.
  Works while the app is open." On by default.
- **Notifications on this device**: press **Enable** to be told about
  tables even when the app is closed. If it says **Blocked**, allow
  notifications for Jokerless in your phone's or browser's settings. On
  iPhone and iPad, using the web version: "add this site to your home screen
  first, then enable notifications there." When notifications are off,
  invites only appear while the app is open.

### Game circle

Account menu › **Game circle** is "The people you tell when you open a table,
and the people who tell you." Both guests and signed-in players can have
one.

- **Your circle**: people who hear about the tables you open for friends.
  **Remove** takes someone out.
- **Requests**: people who'd like to tell you about their tables. You can
  **Accept** or **Decline**.
- **Who can invite you**: **Mute** anyone whose tables you'd rather not hear
  about.
- **Played recently**: people you've shared a table with. Press **Add**.
- **Add someone**: share your friend link ("Anyone who opens your friend link
  joins your circle, and you join theirs"), show it as a QR code, or send a
  request by **Username**, which they have to accept.
